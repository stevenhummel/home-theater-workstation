Measurement interpretation & data gaps

  • Preserve sensitivity test conditions: 2.83 V vs 1 W, distance, frequency averaging window, and anechoic vs in-room.
  • CTA-2034 curves (on-axis, listening window, early reflections, sound power, directivity indices) should drive directivity and room-interaction analysis—not a generic dispersion number alone.
  • Use impedance magnitude/phase for amplifier-load assessment; distortion and compression data for output/headroom. Do not infer these from nominal impedance or sensitivity.
  • Subwoofer model, exact THX-265B independent measurements, and machine-readable curve files remain acquisition tasks. Unknowns are never filled with proxy values.
